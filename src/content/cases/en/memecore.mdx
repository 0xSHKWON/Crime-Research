## Overview

MemeCore (M) is a major token that broke into the top 20 by market cap at around $6.5B. Per ZachXBT's on-chain analysis, however, **roughly 99.6% of the supply is allegedly controlled by insiders**, with the non-insider free float estimated at only **0.1% (~$4M)**. Other manipulation-allegation cases (RAVE, SIREN, LAB) follow a *single vertical pump and abrupt dump* shape; M is different — its market cap **accumulated in stages over nine months** (two distinct pump phases in 2025-09 and 2026-04, separated by a long range).

In this case, ZachXBT explicitly framed the *exchanges* not as neutral venues but as **"co-conspirators"**. On July 3, 2025, the day Kraken listed M on spot, a suspect team wallet reportedly transferred 5.3M M to Kraken deposit addresses, with 18 newly created addresses receiving withdrawals — a flow he visualized using TRM Labs. **This page does not assert that Kraken, Binance, or Bitget participated in any manipulation**; it cites the allegations ZachXBT raised and the exchanges' non-response as stated.

> "Their largest accomplishment is reaching $66M total volume on the app but the token has $6B market cap. lol."
> — ZachXBT

> "Please present a single data point that supports a top 20 token at a $6B market cap. And explain why insiders hold over 90%."
> — ZachXBT, public question to MemeCore (posted right after MemeCore's Grayscale-recognition announcement)

## Supply Structure — Official Disclosure vs On-Chain Analysis

The core allegation in this case is the **~60-percentage-point gap between MemeCore's official tokenomics and ZachXBT's on-chain analysis**.

### MemeCore official distribution (per the project)

| Category | Share |
|---------|------|
| Community | 58% |
| Foundation | 15% |
| Core Contributors | 13% |
| Investors | 12% |
| Meme Treasury | 2% |

By these numbers, insiders (Foundation + Core + Investors) total **40%** — a simple majority, but not at a manipulation threshold.

### ZachXBT on-chain analysis

| Category | Share |
|---------|------|
| Binance Alpha wallets | 48.85% |
| Insider cluster | 23.46% |
| Genesis team wallets | 25.82% |
| **Total insider-controlled** | **~99.6%** |
| Meson Finance + Kraken et al. | ~0.27% |
| Non-insider holdable free float | **~0.1%** |

### What the 60-point gap means

ZachXBT stated that this gap **must be explained**. Possible hypotheses:

1. Tokens classified as "Community" may have actually been distributed to the insider cluster
2. Insider wallets disguised as airdrop / incentive program recipients
3. Foundation-controlled multisigs split across many wallets to evade counting

This page does not assert which hypothesis is correct; with no official MemeCore response, the *gap itself* is the strongest available circumstance.

### Reported vs actual circulating supply — a 6x discrepancy

An additional allegation:

| Metric | Value |
|------|-----|
| CoinMarketCap / CoinGecko reported | **1.29B M circulating** |
| ZachXBT-estimated actual unlock | **~230M M** |
| Discrepancy | **~6x** |

If accurate, the market cap calculation itself is inflated by a factor of six. CMC and CoinGecko may be receiving incorrect unlock data and over-reporting market cap. That data-validation failure at the indexing layer then flows directly into retail's safety judgment ("a top 20 token should be safe") — an infrastructure problem worth flagging.

### What staged accumulation implies

RAVE was a *9-day pump followed by a 24-hour dump*; SIREN cycled three times within a month. M, by contrast, has unfolded as two pump phases over nine months with a long range in between — a **segmented accumulation** rather than a single vertical move. Implications:

- Because it is segmented rather than a single event, analysts and reporters arrive late
- Even when ZachXBT discloses, price does not crash immediately (insider control is tight; sellers are scarce — about -30% from the ~$4.80 ATH to ~$3.30 after the disclosure)
- Exchange surveillance systems have weaker triggers to flag short-term abnormal patterns
- Insiders can *gradually* distribute during the range phase (2025-10 ~ 2026-02)
- This is **ongoing manipulation** — not a finished event, but something *unfolding now*

M differs from other cases on this site in being a **real-time alert rather than a post-mortem**.

The clearest single set of figures in this case:

| Item | Value |
|------|-----|
| MemeCore app cumulative trading volume (including incentivized campaigns) | $66M |
| M token market cap | $6B |

For comparison, a normal top 20 token's market-cap-to-daily-volume ratio is 20-50:1 (Bitcoin ~25:1, Ethereum ~30:1). M stands at **~91:1 on cumulative app volume, and 200:1 or more on daily volume**.

Note the emphasis on "total": *cumulative* trading volume is $66M — and that figure is itself inflated by MemeCore's incentivized InfoFi (reward) campaigns. Even so, it caps out there.

## Lessons — Signals to identify similar cases

M is a *staged-accumulation*-shaped allegation case with a different rhythm from others, so the identifying signals are also different.

### "Major-token credibility trap" signals

- **Sits in the top 20-30 by market cap, yet fundamentals are unclear** — market cap itself is not evidence of legitimacy
- **Large gaps between CMC / CoinGecko reported figures and actual unlocks** — a 6x discrepancy is a strong red flag
- **Self-justification via "elite recognition" such as Grayscale or exchange listings** — recognition does not imply due diligence

### Volume-ratio signals (the clearest set)

- **A market-cap-to-cumulative-app-volume ratio of 50:1 or higher** — highly abnormal
- **A market-cap-to-daily-volume ratio of 100:1 or higher** — effectively a non-traded asset
- **Volume inflated by incentivized campaigns** — verify whether the "real users" figure is propped up by reward incentives

### Distribution-vs-control gap signals

- **A large gap between official tokenomics and on-chain analysis** — the strongest single signal
- **The "Community" category potentially being an insider cluster in practice** — labeling does not guarantee truth
- **Airdrops / incentive distributions concentrated across many newly created addresses** — a trace of distributed accumulation by insiders

### Listing-day pattern signals

- **Team wallet sending large amounts to exchanges *on the listing day itself*** — a trace of pre-coordinated activity
- **Heavy buying by newly created addresses right after listing** — pre-positioned insiders
- **Price action between the listing announcement and the open** — a normal market needs time for price discovery

### Forensic-tool usage

- **Visualizations from TRM Labs · Chainalysis and similar tools** — they expose fund-flow patterns at a glance
- **Exchange hot wallet → many newly created addresses pattern** — a trace of distribution-side identity laundering

### Project-response signals

- **CEO / team publicly mocking critics** — not a normal business response. A sign of confidence or desperation
- **Ignoring criticism without issuing a denial** — in contrast with the RAVE team's denial thread
- **Synchronized non-response from exchanges** — a signal that exchanges, too, cannot settle on a position

### Exchange-credibility signals

- **An exchange that joins a sanctions / illicit-finance-prevention campaign while continuing to list suspect tokens** — internal inconsistency
- **Undisclosed listing due-diligence process** — no way to explain how the listing was approved
- **Continued non-response even after public disclosure** — surveillance system not operating

## Industry implications

1. **The lifespan of pump-and-dump is extending.** Traditional pump-and-dumps run in days; M is a 9-month staged accumulation (first pump → range → second pump). Disclosures land late, retail enters as *slow* inelastic buyers and stack up, and exchange surveillance loses the trigger to flag any single abnormality. The model unfolds *while analysts wait for a clear event*.
2. **Exchange sanctimony.** That ZachXBT disclosed the M case the same day Kraken announced support for Operation Atlantic is not coincidence. It is a direct challenge to exchanges' "compliance theater."
3. **The accountability of indexing sites.** CMC and CoinGecko display 1.29B M as circulating (estimated actual ~230M). Even when the market cap is inflated sixfold, retail decides "a top 20 token should be safe." The indexing layer's data-validation failure becomes part of the infrastructure of alleged manipulation.
4. **A CEO's social-media behavior as forensic evidence.** When publicly mocking critics is consistent with the manipulation allegations, that behavior pattern becomes data, not noise.
5. **Escalation.** ZachXBT's ICE-report warning signals more than disclosure — *potential law-enforcement follow-through*. Manipulation cases are escalating into law-enforcement territory.
