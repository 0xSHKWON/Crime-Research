## Overview

A +10,383% run in nine days, then a -95% collapse in twenty-four hours. Roughly $52M in liquidations was enough to evaporate $5.7B of market cap.

The structural setup is a token with no Binance Spot listing but with Binance Alpha and Binance Futures live in parallel. A team-affiliated multisig that allegedly held about 90% of supply cycled deposits in and out of exchanges, building a short-squeeze environment. The price ran from $0.25 to $27.94 and then unwound 95% in a single day. After ZachXBT's public disclosure, Binance, Bitget, and Gate each opened a formal investigation.

> "A 52M-dollar liquidation that vaporizes 6B in market cap is not the kind of ratio a healthy market produces." — paraphrased from ZachXBT (X, 2026-04-18)

## Supply Structure

### Market structure — why this setup was possible

The core of this case is a **structural gap: Binance Spot is missing**.

| Exchange | Spot | Perpetual | Alpha |
|----------|------|-----------|-------|
| **Binance** | ❌ | O | O |
| Bitget | O | O | — |
| Gate.io | O | O | — |
| KuCoin | O | — | — |
| Coinbase | ❌ | ❌ | — |

The mark price for Binance perpetuals does not reference an in-house spot book. Instead, it references a spot index built from external exchanges — Bitget, Gate, and KuCoin. Those external books carry thin liquidity, so relatively small flows can move the mark price. A moved mark price, in turn, becomes the trigger that liquidates the multi-billion-dollar open interest sitting on Binance perpetuals.

### Supply distribution — free float is in the low single digits

1B tokens are issued, but the top 3 wallets — all Gnosis Safe contracts, allegedly team-affiliated per ZachXBT's analysis — hold 91.06%. After adding the exchange deposit addresses (Bitget, Gate, KuCoin), the share that actually circulates in the market falls to a low single-digit percentage.

## How It Worked — The Short-Squeeze Setup

### Stage 1 — Bait the shorts with deposit-then-withdraw cycling

Team-affiliated wallets reportedly deposit roughly $42M worth of RAVE into Bitget. The market reads this as "the team is about to dump", and short positions accumulate (at the peak, around 74% of Binance traders are positioned short).

Roughly $32M is then withdrawn back on-chain. The expected sell pressure disappears from the order book, leaving the book with bid pressure only.

### Stage 2 — Liquidation cascade

As price runs higher, forced short liquidations begin. The liquidation engine's automatic market-buy orders push price further up, which triggers the next band of liquidations — a self-reinforcing loop.

Inside this mechanism, an insider barely needs to sell spot to walk price higher. The cascade does the work of pushing the price.

### Stage 3 — Layered deposit at the peak

At 2026-04-19 19:47:59 UTC, in a single transaction, the multisig `0x53d7...487b` split its entire **22,996,077 RAVE** balance into two paths. Both paths, within one to six minutes, forwarded onward into another set of burner wallets (each path used the small **test** transfer first, then a **bulk** transfer of the remainder — the standard SOP for validating an exchange deposit). Within 11 minutes, every dollar of that flow had converged into the exchange hot wallet `Bitget 6` (`0x1ab4...8f23`).

Notably, all of the intermediate burner wallets had been activated using ETH gas sent from `Bitget 6` itself (Etherscan's "Funded By" tag). Because the funder and the final receiver are the same address, the layered deposits are most plausibly read as **a single Bitget account holder deliberately designing the routing** — that is the allegation.

In the eleven minutes following this layered deposit, price dropped a further 40%, from about $1 to $0.60.

## Lessons — Signals to identify similar tokens

These are the risk indicators a trader can pull from this case. Any single one is weak on its own, but **three or more in combination is a strong warning signal**.

### Market-structure signals

- No Binance Spot listing while Binance Alpha and perpetuals run in parallel
- Thin spot venues (Bitget, Gate, KuCoin) act as the primary price-discovery layer
- A newly issued token is listed quickly across multiple exchanges

### Supply-structure signals

- The top 3 to 10 wallets hold 85%+ of supply
- Circulating supply sits at 25% or less of total
- No clear vesting or lockup schedule is published
- Gnosis Safe multisigs hold a large share

### Price-action signals

- A sharp run without an identifiable catalyst (no news, no partnership, no upgrade)
- Abnormal market-cap to volume ratio (e.g. $1B cap on under $50M of daily volume)
- **Market cap to liquidation ratio above 100:1** — the clearest single indicator in RAVE

### Derivatives signals

- OI / market cap above 0.3
- Abnormal funding (sustained at ±0.05% per 8h or more)
- Long/short ratio skewed more than 70% to one side

### On-chain signals

- Large deposits from team-affiliated wallets to exchanges just before a pump
- Large withdrawals from exchanges back to team-affiliated wallets after a pump (reclaiming the apparent sell pressure)
- Contract admin privileges that have not been renounced

### ZachXBT's own caution

> "I also do not recommend shorting suspected manipulation tokens with concentrated supply."

A short-squeeze setup can be re-run at any time, which means that any token with high insider concentration is dangerous to position in — long or short. The safest choice, by a wide margin, is **not to trade it**.
