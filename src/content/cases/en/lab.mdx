## Overview

~+350% in 72 hours to a $6B FDV, then −65% within hours. Roughly three weeks after the RAVE crash (April 2026), the same pattern repeated on the same exchange infrastructure.

LAB is a BNB Chain token marketed as an "AI multi-chain trading terminal." It had the no-Binance-spot + alpha/perp structure, an extremely concentrated supply, and a short early-May pump that reversed within hours. ZachXBT alleged that insiders control more than 95% of the supply in coordination with an unknown market maker and Bitget, and posted a $10K bounty.

This token is unrelated to the 2021 real-estate NFT project "LABS Group" — it is a separate token (contract `0x7ec4…593a`, BNB Chain).

> "An unknown MM operating via Chinese exchanges" — ZachXBT argued the same playbook seen in RAVE, RIVER and SIREN was repeated with LAB.

We indexed this contract ourselves to verify ZachXBT's key figures. The results appear in each section below.

## Supply structure

### Market structure — the same "no spot" flaw as RAVE

| Exchange | Spot | Perpetual | Alpha |
|----------|------|-----------|-------|
| **Binance** | ❌ | O | O |
| Bitget | O | — | — |
| Bybit | — | O | — |
| OKX | — | O | — |
| Gate.io | O | — | — |
| KuCoin | O | — | — |

Binance has no LAB spot. The perp's mark price references the thin spot indices of outside exchanges (Bitget, Gate, KuCoin), and the market-making activity ZachXBT cited was framed across "Bitget spot · Bybit perp · Binance perp · OKX perp." Move the shallow external spot with little capital, and perps on several venues move together. The same flaw seen in RAVE.

### Supply distribution — our own measurement

ZachXBT claimed insiders control more than 95% of the supply. We indexed every transfer of the BNB Chain contract `0x7ec4…593a` and computed net balances as of 2026-05-14.

- The top 6 wallets hold **84.3%** of the 1B total supply
- The top 20 wallets hold roughly **97.8%**
- The single largest wallet holds 20.0% (a round 200M LAB)

The top ranks mix round-number large holders (200M, 150M, 138M, 108M) with Bitget hot wallets. The freely circulating float measures in the single-digit percentages. The external "95%" figure uses the reported circulating supply (309.95M) as its denominator while our measurement uses total supply (1B) — different denominators, but both lead to the same conclusion: extreme concentration.

## Method analysis — layered withdrawal via Bitget

### The same addresses show up again

The most striking finding is address reuse. In our own measurement, the LAB top holders include **the exact same exchange addresses seen in the RAVE case**.

- `0xffa8db7b…cd54` — the hot wallet labeled "Bitget 35" in RAVE. Holds 15.34% of LAB.
- `0x1ab4973a…8f23` — "Bitget 6" in RAVE. In LAB it is the **source** that funded the fresh-wallet cluster.
- `0x0d070796…92fe` — the same address as RAVE's "Gate.io deposit" and ARIA's Gate.io hot wallet.
- `0x73d8bd54…46db` — a cross-token wallet also observed in SIREN and ARIA.

This is consistent with ZachXBT's description of "the same market maker operating via Chinese exchanges" sharing one infrastructure across LAB, RAVE, SIREN and ARIA.

### Fresh-wallet cluster — own measurement

ZachXBT said ~100M LAB (~$480M) left Bitget for 10 fresh wallets on May 11-12. Our measurement: a single Bitget hot wallet (`0x1ab4…8f23`) distributed **~91.4M LAB across 18 transfers to 9 fresh wallets between 2026-05-08 and 05-12**, roughly evenly (~9-11M each). The final batch was at May 12 00:57 UTC. The source being a Bitget hot wallet, and the timing overlapping the peak, both match the external account.

## Lessons — signals to spot the next one

LAB shares nearly every risk factor with RAVE. A single item is weak, but **several overlapping ones are a strong warning**. In this case the strongest signal was "are addresses from a prior incident showing up again."

### Market-structure signals

- No Binance spot + alpha/perp structure (price discovery depends on a thin external spot)
- Rapid distribution across multiple exchange perps right after TGE
- Market-making venues split across one spot + several perps

### Supply-structure signals

- A few top wallets hold 80%+ of total supply (LAB: top 6 = 84.3%)
- The reported circulating supply itself is low — around 30% of total
- Presence of round-number large holders (200M, 150M)
- Lockup/vesting terms changeable unilaterally after the fact (admin rights not renounced)

### On-chain signals (the crux here)

- **Large layered withdrawal from an exchange hot wallet to fresh wallets right before the pump** (LAB: Bitget → 9 fresh wallets, May 8-12)
- Fresh wallets receiving near-identical amounts simultaneously (coordination signal)
- **Exchange / intermediary addresses from prior suspected-manipulation cases reappearing** — RAVE's Bitget 6/35, Gate.io, and a cross-token wallet all show up in LAB's top ranks

### Price / derivatives signals

- A short, catalyst-free +350% spike that reverses within hours
- Multi-billion-dollar FDV while the actually tradable float is single-digit %
- A credible trader/analyst posting a public sell warning beforehand (LAB: a prominent investor, May 10)

### Trading judgment

> "Shorting a manipulation-suspected token with concentrated supply isn't advisable either."

Whether it is a short-squeeze setup like RAVE, or a withdraw-then-distribute pattern like LAB, a token with high insider concentration is dangerous to position in either direction. The safest choice is **not to trade it**.
